Add experimental per-installment hold readback (feature id `media_buy.installment_holds`, #8086). A committed canonical proposal whose `hold_policy.available` is `true` may carry `holds`, which lists each installment the hold covers. Each entry has a `product_id` from `commercial_terms.purchases` and an `installment_ref`, and appears once. `holds` is readback of the one all-or-none hold that finalize placed, not a set of partial holds. Entries share `hold_placed_at` and the proposal's `expires_at`, have no `expires_at` of their own, and are accepted, extended, or lapse only with the proposal. Under a rankable policy (`media_buy.hold_ranking`), every entry carries the buyer's own `hold_rank` for that installment, and the proposal's `hold_rank` is no better than any entry's. Entries are readback, not contract: each installment belongs to its product and falls within the purchase's flight, and `commercial_terms` stays authoritative. A seller that lists the feature includes `holds` on every committed proposal that allocates a product declaring specific installments, and never describes other buyers' holds. To hold fewer installments, the buyer declines or lets the committed proposal lapse and finalizes a revised draft. `extend_hold` successors list the same entries at positions no worse than before, and never move `expires_at` past an installment booking deadline or change cutoff. `holds` is forbidden on drafts and terms-only commitments, and omitted for callers below 3.3. Adds `installment_holds` vectors and grader codes to `test-vectors/hold-policy`.
