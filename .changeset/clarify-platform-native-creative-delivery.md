Docs: show how AdCP 3.3 reports and adopts creatives the buyer didn't sync, such as ads an advertiser made in a self-serve platform's own tools (adcp#8897). `get_media_buy_delivery` now explains that such a creative's `by_creative` row carries the seller-assigned `creative_id` that `list_creatives` returns, with the platform's identifier in `platform_id`, and that the ID is stable when the seller declares `creative.stable_creative_ids`. It also shows how to read one creative's delivery day by day with one-day reads, and how a buyer joins delivery before and after a `merge`. A new section states that daily reach and frequency don't add up to period reach. The creative-level section also states what a `by_creative` row already means: the delivery the seller measured for that creative, not the package total divided across creatives, which is why `weight` is an observed share. `sync_creatives` gains a walkthrough for adopting such a creative with `merge` and names the two 3.3 limits: merge is per package, and earlier delivery stays under the seller's ID. Schema descriptions for `by_creative[].creative_id`, `by_creative`, the creative delivery row, `supports_creative_breakdown`, `by_spot[].creative_id` and both `daily_breakdown` arrays now say the same. Wire behavior is unchanged.
