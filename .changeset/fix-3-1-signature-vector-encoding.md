Re-encode two request-signing Signature tokens as unpadded base64url so they satisfy the maintained 3.1 emission profile.

`positive/002-post-with-content-digest.json` and `negative/018-digest-covered-when-forbidden.json` were re-signed with padded standard base64 when operation-valid bodies were backported. `docs/building/by-layer/L1/security.mdx` ("Binary value encoding") overrides RFC 8941 for this profile: `Signature` MUST be emitted as base64url without padding, and a signer emitting `+`, `/` or `=` is non-conformant. Verifiers only SHOULD lenient-decode pure standard base64, so a conforming strict verifier may reject these with `request_signature_header_malformed` — which is what the maintained SDK does on an explicit 3.1 pin. They were the only 2 of the 37 signed vectors in the standard alphabet. Of the remaining 36 tokens, 17 are demonstrably base64url (they carry `-` or `_`) and the rest are alphabet-neutral dummy signatures that render identically under either alphabet.

The effect on an explicitly 3.1-pinned verifier: `positive/002` failed at step 1 instead of verifying, and `negative/018` failed at step 1 for the wrong reason instead of reaching its intended `request_signature_components_unexpected` at step 6. Unpinned verifiers accept both alphabets, which is why existing coverage missed it.

Only the two `Signature` tokens change. Each decodes to the same 64 signature bytes and is re-encoded, so nothing is re-signed: request bodies, `Content-Digest`, `Signature-Input`, `expected_signature_base` and the Ed25519 key material are untouched. `Content-Digest` keeps its historical standard-base64 form. The same `security.mdx` rule names `Content-Digest` alongside `Signature`, so that form is a known discrepancy tracked separately — this change neither resolves it nor claims to. It is deferred to keep this change a pure re-encode: `content-digest` is a covered component, so its literal field value is part of `expected_signature_base`, and changing it would mean a genuine re-sign rather than a re-encode.

`tests/request-signing-vector-bodies.test.cjs` asserted the RFC 8941 default rather than the AdCP override. It now checks the unpadded-base64url grammar with a canonical round-trip and compares the deterministic Ed25519 re-sign in the same encoding. A new corpus guard applies that grammar to every `Signature` token in all 37 signed vectors (38 tokens), with the 3 deliberately unsigned vectors allowlisted and both counts pinned so a new vector cannot silently skip it. On the 21 alphabet-neutral tokens the guard necessarily enforces only the no-padding and canonical-trailing-bits half of the grammar, which is stated inline. The truthful `Content-Digest` check is unchanged, with its scope stated inline.

The two encoding profiles are separated by directory, not by branch. These root `positive/` and `negative/` directories are the legacy 3.0/3.1 corpus on both lines — on main the same root vectors are already unpadded base64url, so this change brings 3.1.x's two outliers into line with them rather than diverging from main. The standard-alphabet 3.2 corpus lives in the sibling `profile-3.2/` directory. This base64url grammar must NOT be applied to `profile-3.2/`, where the standard alphabet is correct. No blanket take-ours/take-theirs claim about these paths is intended.
