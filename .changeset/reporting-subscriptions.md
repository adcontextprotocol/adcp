Add experimental principal-level reporting subscriptions under the new feature id `media_buy.reporting_subscriptions` (RFC #8543, Part B). Reliable Reporting was configured one account at a time through `sync_accounts` `reporting_delivery_configs`, so every new account reported nothing until someone configured it. `sync_principal` gains a `reporting_subscriptions` section (`core/reporting-subscription.json`): a subscription scoped to `all_authorized_accounts: true` (an explicit acknowledgment) or `account_ids[]`, with optional `exclude_account_ids[]`, a cross-seller `selector` (`feed_purpose`, `period_duration`, `required_finality`, optional `dimensions[]`, `match: finest | all`, and `period_timezone` for subscriptions scoped to exactly one account), `reconciliation_mode`, and an optional `destination` naming a principal `reporting_destinations` entry with a `path_template` that must contain `{account_id}` when the scope can cover more than one account. `subscription_version` changes only when delivery semantics change. `get_principal` and `sync_principal` read subscriptions back through `core/reporting-subscription-state.json`.

The seller resolves each subscription per account into ordinary obligations whose `delivery_config_id` is the `subscription_id`; obligations gain an optional `offering_id` naming the resolved offering, and a resolution change applies from the next period boundary. Coverage is computable by the buyer without asking: in every account the principal is authorized for, a confirmed buy is owed every complete period of the resolved offering's schedule, in the account's timezone, that starts on or after the later of the subscription version's `accepted_at` (readback, with `prior_versions`) and the buy's `confirmed_at`, through its final period; periods are never clipped. This refines RFC #8543 B1, which listed authorization start as a third term and counted periods ending after the start. `get_reporting_status` summary and periods views gain `subscription_coverage[]` (`core/reporting-subscription-coverage.json`), which reports only exceptions, each removing periods over a `[from, until)` boundary interval and never adding any: `excluded[]` entries per account or buy with a reason (`offering_unsupported`, `capacity`, `overridden` with the overriding `delivery_config_id` or `subscription_id`, `excluded_by_buyer`, `action_required` with a `pending_action`), and unbackfilled `gaps[]` after a re-grant. Precedence: an active `sync_accounts` `reporting_delivery_configs` entry for an account and feed purpose wins over any subscription, and an `account_ids` subscription wins over an all-accounts one. `get_reporting_status` caller isolation is unchanged: when the caller loses authority for an account, the subscription's `get_principal` state lists it under `accounts_unauthorized[]` (`unauthorized_at`, `last_owed_period_end`, `withheld_period_count`, optional withheld obligation IDs) for `status_retention_days` or until re-grant. Withheld periods are owed again on re-grant, due at re-grant plus the offering's `delivery_sla`; with `backfill_on_regrant` no gap is recorded and the revoked interval stays owed. `sync_reporting_receipts` and `sync_reporting_status` keep accepting receipts and statuses for revisions the caller read before `unauthorized_at`, for `status_retention_days`. `sync_accounts` rejects a `delivery_config_id` equal to one of the caller's `subscription_id` values.

Adds the extensible `enums/reporting-dimension.json` vocabulary (`package`, `creative`, `country`, `region`, `metro`). `media_buy.reporting_delivery` gains `configuration_tasks[]` (for example `["sync_principal", "sync_accounts"]`) and an experimental `subscriptions` block (`supported`, `max_accounts`, `backfill_on_regrant`, `dimensions`). `configuration_task` keeps its `const: "sync_accounts"` value and is now marked deprecated. `adcp.principal.supported_sections` gains `reporting_subscriptions`. Advertising subscriptions requires the `media_buy.reporting_subscriptions` feature id, the `reporting_subscriptions` section, and `sync_principal` in `configuration_tasks`.
