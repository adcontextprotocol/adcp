Add experimental principal-level reporting subscriptions under the new feature id `media_buy.reporting_subscriptions` (RFC #8543, Part B). Reliable Reporting was configured one account at a time through `sync_accounts` `reporting_delivery_configs`, so every new account reported nothing until someone configured it. `sync_principal` gains a `reporting_subscriptions` section (`core/reporting-subscription.json`): a subscription scoped to `all_authorized_accounts: true` (an explicit acknowledgment) or `account_ids[]`, with optional `exclude_account_ids[]`, a cross-seller `selector` (`feed_purpose`, `period_duration`, `required_finality`, optional `dimensions[]`, `match: finest | all`, and `period_timezone` for account-scoped subscriptions), `reconciliation_mode`, and an optional `destination` naming a principal `reporting_destinations` entry with a `path_template` that must contain `{account_id}` when the scope can cover more than one account. `subscription_version` changes only when delivery semantics change. `get_principal` and `sync_principal` read subscriptions back through `core/reporting-subscription-state.json`.

The seller resolves each subscription per account into ordinary obligations whose `delivery_config_id` is the `subscription_id`. `get_reporting_status` summary and periods views gain `subscription_coverage[]` (`core/reporting-subscription-coverage.json`): per subscription, the account `status` (`active`, `excluded`, `unauthorized` with `unauthorized_at`, `action_required` with a `pending_action`), `covered[]` buys with an immutable `covered_from`, optional `covered_until`, and the resolved `offering_id` and `report_definition_id`, `excluded[]` entries with a reason (`offering_unsupported`, `unauthorized`, `overridden` with the overriding `subscription_id` or `delivery_config_id`, `capacity`, `excluded_by_buyer`), `withheld_unauthorized[]` periods, and unbackfilled `gaps[]` after a re-grant. The more specific scope wins per account and feed purpose; during 3.x an account-level `reporting_delivery_configs` entry counts as account-scoped. A principal that lost authority may still read the summary view for that account for `status_retention_days`.

Adds the extensible `enums/reporting-dimension.json` vocabulary (`package`, `creative`, `country`, `region`, `metro`). `media_buy.reporting_delivery` gains `configuration_tasks[]` (for example `["sync_principal", "sync_accounts"]`) and an experimental `subscriptions` block (`supported`, `max_accounts`, `backfill_on_regrant`, `dimensions`). `configuration_task` keeps its `const: "sync_accounts"` value and is now marked deprecated. `adcp.principal.supported_sections` gains `reporting_subscriptions`. Advertising subscriptions requires the `media_buy.reporting_subscriptions` feature id, the `reporting_subscriptions` section, and `sync_principal` in `configuration_tasks`.
