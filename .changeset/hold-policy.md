Add an experimental `hold_policy` object (`core/hold-policy.json`, feature id `media_buy.hold_policy`, #7661) so buyers can plan an inventory hold before finalizing. It declares `available`, a promised `min_duration`, an initial `max_duration`, `extendable`, and `max_total_duration` (strictly positive ISO 8601 durations in days, hours, minutes, and seconds; durations and `extendable` are forbidden when `available` is false). On `product` and `canonical-product` it states what the product supports; on a draft `proposal` or `canonical-proposal` it states what a successful finalize will get, resolved from the allocated products, and the committed successor carries it forward unchanged. A successful finalize MUST set `expires_at` no earlier than when the hold is placed plus `min_duration`, otherwise the entry returns `hold_unavailable` with `batch_aborted` siblings. If any allocated product is non-holdable, the whole proposal finalizes terms only with no partial holds, and the 3.3 version rule for `terms_only` committed proposals applies. `media_buy.proposal_refinement.hold_semantics` becomes the default for products that do not declare `hold_policy` rather than one setting for the whole agent. The policy reserves nothing. When `hold_policy` is absent everywhere, behavior is unchanged.
