Add an experimental `hold_policy` object (`core/hold-policy.json`, feature id `media_buy.hold_policy`, #7661) so buyers can plan an inventory hold before finalizing. It declares `available` (an exclusive, first-position hold), a promised `min_duration`, an initial `max_duration`, `extendable` (default false), and `max_total_duration` (only when `extendable` is true). Durations are strictly positive ISO 8601 values in days, hours, minutes, and seconds, where a day is exactly 24 hours on the UTC instant and durations compare after normalizing. Durations and `extendable` are forbidden when `available` is false. On `product` and `canonical-product` the policy is a planning hint. On a draft `canonical-proposal` it binds: the seller derives it from the allocated products, and the committed successor carries it forward unchanged. Legacy `core/proposal.json` does not carry it. A seller that lists the feature puts `hold_policy` on every draft and committed proposal. A committed proposal that holds inventory adds `hold_placed_at`, which lies between request receipt and the response or `submitted` completion. A successful finalize MUST satisfy `hold_placed_at + min_duration <= expires_at <= hold_placed_at + max_duration`, and the hold never runs past an installment booking deadline or change cutoff. Otherwise the entry returns `hold_unavailable` with `batch_aborted` siblings, and an exact retry never restarts the clock. A non-holdable draft finalizes terms only with no partial holds, and #7954's `terms_only` rules, including the 3.3 version rule, now apply per finalize entry. `media_buy.proposal_refinement.hold_semantics` becomes the default for products and drafts that do not declare `hold_policy`. Sellers omit `hold_policy` and `hold_placed_at` for callers below 3.3. Adds reference test vectors under `test-vectors/hold-policy`. When `hold_policy` is absent everywhere, behavior is unchanged.
