Add experimental seller offers against an accepted media buy (#8087; absorbs the offer-and-accept loop of #5683). `core/canonical-proposal.json` gains optional `proposal_initiator` (`buyer | seller`, new `enums/proposal-initiator.json`; absent means `buyer`) and `offer_reason` (new `enums/offer-reason.json`: `installment_postponed | installment_cancelled | preemption | under_delivery | seller_request`). A seller-initiated proposal MUST carry `offer_reason`, a `proposal_id` matching the notification id pattern, and be a `media_buy_update` or `media_buy_cancellation` that is `committed` (`accepted` once the buyer accepts); the existing kind conditional still requires `parent_proposal_id`, `media_buy_id` and `base_media_buy_revision`. Offers are read through `list_proposals` and announced by the new account-anchored, identifiers-only `proposal.offered` notification (`core/proposal-offered-webhook.json`: `media_buy_id`, `proposal_id`, `expires_at`, `offer_reason`; `notification_id` equals `proposal_id`), a sibling of `proposal.created`; both depend on `media_buy.open_opportunities` (#8103) and a seller declaring `media_buy.seller_offers` MUST also declare `media_buy.open_opportunities` and advertise `list_proposals`. An offer carries no `opportunity_id`. `proposal.offered` is accepted by `notification-config.json` and `agent-notification-config.json`.

The buyer accepts with `accept_proposal`, declines with `decline_proposals`, or revises with `refine_proposals`, which yields a buyer-initiated draft. `expires_at` is the response deadline and the seller holds replacement inventory until then. At `expires_at` the offer's disposition is `expired`, `accept_proposal` returns `PROPOSAL_EXPIRED`, and the signed terms stand; silence is never acceptance. The cluster is `media_buy.seller_offers` in the experimental registry. Seller proposals into a buyer-opened open opportunity are `media_buy.open_opportunities` (#8096, #8103), not this surface.

A second experimental lane, `media_buy.seller_adjustment_allowances`, models pre-agreed seller leeway on a live buy by reusing `commercial_terms.change_terms[]` instead of a second vocabulary. `change-term.json` gains `holder` (`buyer | seller`, default `buyer`) and an optional `purchase_index` (required on a seller-held term). A seller-held term is limited to `decrease_budget`, `update_impression_goal`, `update_flight_dates` and `update_targeting`, and `change-term-constraints.json` gains the `volume` (`min_result_impressions`) and `alternates` (an ordered chain of complete replacement `targeting_overlay` values) kinds. Every seller-held term needs at least one absolute result bound (`min_result_amount`, `min_result_impressions`, `earliest_result` or `latest_result`, or `alternates`); relative and fraction bounds are rejected, so bounds cannot compound. The seller states the terms in the committed proposal and the buyer binds them through `accept_proposal` and `terms_digest`, as with `cancellation_terms`. Within a bound the seller applies the change as a new media buy revision with no offer; anything else goes to the offer lane. A seller MUST NOT lengthen a `time`-priced purchase or shorten a `flat_rate` one, and decreasing a purchase `budget` decreases `total_budget`, when present, by the same amount. Each application mints an accepted successor proposal (`offer_reason` gains `term_applied`, which is never an offer and never `committed`) and makes every committed proposal against the prior revision stale, whichever party initiated it: an outstanding seller offer is listed `withdrawn` under #8103, and the buyer's own committed amendment fails the base-revision check and loses its hold. Each application is listed in `get_media_buys` `applied_adjustments[]` (new `core/applied-seller-adjustment.json`: `term_id`, `accepted_proposal_id`, `action`, `previous_value`, `value`), the revision history entry records the origin with the existing account change feed vocabulary (`actor` is `seller_system`, or `seller_operator` when a person applied it, matching `origin.kind`) plus `term_id` and a machine-readable `reason` code, the same `term_id` and `reason` are carried on the `core/account-change.json` record, and every application advances the buy's `revision` (companion RFC #8142). A new account-anchored `media_buy.allowance_applied` notification (`core/allowance-applied-webhook.json`, `notification_id` equals `adjustment_id`) is accepted by `notification-config.json` and `agent-notification-config.json`. With no seller-held term the seller has no unilateral leeway. Splitting a package or adding a $0 make-good package is out of scope.

Compatibility: sellers MUST NOT send offers, `proposal.offered` notifications, seller-held `change_terms[]` entries, `holder`, `purchase_index`, `applied_adjustments[]`, the new history fields, or `media_buy.allowance_applied` to callers pinned below AdCP 3.3, because the 3.2 `canonical-proposal` and `commercial-terms` schemas are closed; a caller with no declared pin is treated as pinned below 3.3.
