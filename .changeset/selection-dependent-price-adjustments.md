Add optional `price_adjustments` to binding fixed-price pricing options (`cpm`, `vcpm`, `cpc`, `cpcv`, `cpv`, `cpp`, `flat_rate`) and to `canonical-pricing-option.json` (#7659). The surface is experimental under the feature id `media_buy.price_adjustments`. A table is optional and has equal standing with a fully priced seller offer: whether a table is present tells the buyer if it can compute the price locally, and without one the price applies only to what was quoted; yield-managed sellers are not expected to publish a table. A seller whose rate depends on buyer-selected values or on the delivery date declares an ordered table of `fee`/`discount` rows, each with a `rate` (a proportion of the running total; a fee rate may be 1 or more, so a Dutch index-card factor `f` is a fee rate of `f - 1` or a discount rate of `1 - f`) or a fixed `amount`. Rows are keyed by `format_option` (a `format_option_ref`, which also carries spot length), `placement_selection` (a `placement_ref`, requiring `overlay_support.placement_selection`), or `always`, and may carry a `valid_from`/`valid_until` window evaluated at the purchase `start_time`; a flight crossing a window boundary is rejected. Resolution is normative: selection rows fire at most once per dimension (first applicable row in declared order), then each firing row applies to one running total in declared order (a 10% fee then a 10% fee is x1.21), every step rounds half away from zero to the option's price precision (the greatest of the currency minor-unit exponent, 4 decimals for per-unit models, and the decimals needed to represent declared values exactly, by numeric value), and the result is never negative. The `price_breakdown` rounding sentence is amended to match. A table is bound to its quote: it applies only to the account it was returned for and expires with the proposal or offer `expires_at` or the discovery response's validity. The accepted purchase's `pricing.fixed_price` is the resolved rate, `base_fixed_price` carries the declared rate, and `price_breakdown` records each fired row in declared order so a verifier can re-resolve the snapshot; the whole table is retained and covered by `terms_digest` so a change the agreement already permits is priced from it without a re-quote, but the table grants no change rights (those come only from `change_terms[]`). A changed table mints a new `pricing_option_id`, and a buyer-supplied `pricing` that differs from the resolved result is rejected with `INVALID_REQUEST` carrying the resolved price and breakdown (`error-details/price-adjustments-mismatch.json`). Sellers must not return an option carrying the table to callers pinned below 3.3. The uniform-price language in `Product.pricing_options`, `overlay_support`, and the product, targeting, and pricing docs is amended to except declared adjustments. v1 dimensions are placement and format; buyer-owned property and collection list references have no seller-side anchor and are out, as are auction floors, activation method, measurement vendor, reporting tier, and the `cpa`/`time` models. Adds resolution vectors, including a fictional Dutch-style TV rate card, under `test-vectors/price-adjustments`.
