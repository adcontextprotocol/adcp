Add optional `price_adjustments` to binding fixed-price pricing options (`cpm`, `vcpm`, `cpc`, `cpcv`, `cpv`, `cpp`, `flat_rate`) and to `canonical-pricing-option.json` (#7659). A seller whose rate depends on buyer-selected values or on the delivery date declares an ordered table of rows of two kinds: **index** rows (`factor`, a multiplier compounded in declared order, as on Dutch linear TV rate cards) and **premium** rows (`fee`/`discount` with a `rate` of the indexed price or a fixed `amount`, added). Rows are keyed by `format_option` (a `format_option_ref`, which also carries spot length), `placement_selection` (a `placement_ref`, requiring `overlay_support.placement_selection`), or `always`, and may carry a `valid_from`/`valid_until` window evaluated at the purchase `start_time`; a flight crossing a window boundary is rejected. Resolution is normative: selection rows fire at most once per dimension (first applicable row in declared order), `indexed price = fixed_price x product of factors`, `resolved price = indexed price + premium deltas` (premiums never compound), every delta rounds half away from zero to the option's price precision (the greatest of the currency minor-unit exponent, 4 decimals for per-unit models, and the decimals needed to represent declared values exactly, by numeric value), and the result is never negative. The `price_breakdown` rounding sentence is amended to match. The accepted purchase's `pricing.fixed_price` is the resolved rate, `base_fixed_price` carries the declared rate, and `price_breakdown` records each fired row (an index as a fee or discount rate, a premium as its currency amount) so a verifier can re-resolve the snapshot; the whole table is retained and covered by `terms_digest`, a changed table mints a new `pricing_option_id`, and a buyer-supplied `pricing` that differs from the resolved result is rejected with `INVALID_REQUEST` carrying the resolved price and breakdown. Sellers must not return an option carrying the table to callers pinned below 3.3. The uniform-price language in `Product.pricing_options`, `overlay_support`, and the product, targeting, and pricing docs is amended to except declared adjustments. Auction floors, buyer-owned property/collection lists, activation method, measurement vendor, reporting tier, and the `cpa`/`time` models are out of scope for this version. Adds resolution vectors, including a fictional Dutch-style TV rate card, under `test-vectors/price-adjustments`.
