Add optional `price_adjustments` to binding fixed-price pricing options (`cpm`, `vcpm`, `cpc`, `cpcv`, `cpv`, `flat_rate`) and to `canonical-pricing-option.json` (#7659). A seller whose rate depends on buyer-selected values declares an ordered table of `fee`/`discount` rows, each keyed by a typed selector: `format_option` (a `format_option_ref`) or `placement_selection` (a `placement_ref`, requiring `overlay_support.placement_selection`). `fixed_price` stays the base rate. Resolution is normative: matching is full-identity equality, at most one row fires per dimension (the first match in declared order), firing rows apply in declared order to a running total (a 10% fee then a 10% fee multiplies by 1.21), rounding is half away from zero to the option's price precision after each row, and the total is never negative. The accepted purchase's `pricing.fixed_price` is the resolved rate, `base_fixed_price` carries the declared rate, and `price_breakdown` records the fired rows, so a verifier can re-resolve the snapshot; the whole table is retained and covered by `terms_digest`, a changed table mints a new `pricing_option_id`, and a buyer-supplied `pricing` that differs from the resolved result is rejected with `INVALID_REQUEST`. Sellers must not return an option carrying the table to callers pinned below 3.3. The uniform-price language in `Product.pricing_options`, `overlay_support`, and the product, targeting, and pricing docs is amended to except declared adjustments. Auction floors, buyer-owned property/collection lists, activation method, measurement vendor, reporting tier, and the `cpa`/`cpp`/`time` models are out of scope for this version. Adds resolution vectors under `test-vectors/price-adjustments`.
