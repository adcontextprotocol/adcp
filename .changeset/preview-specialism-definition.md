docs(compliance): say what `preview` specialism status actually means, and when a preview specialism is promoted

The compliance catalog and conformance page described `preview` as a placeholder storyboard that the runner grades as `passed: null`, with badges showing a distinct indicator. That is no longer true: ten of the eleven preview storyboards are fully authored and graded pass or fail, and what preview actually changes is that the claim does not count toward AAO Verified badge issuance or eligibility. The definition now says that, adds the criteria for promoting a preview specialism to `stable`, and the `sponsored-intelligence` placeholder narrative no longer cites the `passed: null` contract.
