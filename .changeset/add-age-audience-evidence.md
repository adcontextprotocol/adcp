Add compact product age evidence and buyer evidence requirements. Products can state an age range, estimated audience share, and whether the estimate is based on verified, declared, inferred, or aggregate population data; required discovery rules fail closed, preferred rules affect ranking, and matching evidence is copied into package readback.

Add basis-aware age targeting without weakening the canonical age predicate. Buyers can constrain `demographics.age.accepted_bases`, products declare user-level `supported_bases`, and package readback records effective applied bases and verification methods. Aggregate `population_estimate` evidence is structurally unavailable for per-user execution, age compliance continues to override demographic permissions, and World ID threshold claims must entail the requested age predicate. This new capability should be reflected in future media-buy certification coverage.
