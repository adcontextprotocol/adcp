Stop `creative/policy_backed_rejections` failing sellers that do not enforce registry policies (adcp#8850). The storyboard seeds a product with `enforced_policies` through the controller and then asserted that the seller published and enforced both fixture policies, so a seller that never claimed registry policy enforcement failed it. The 3.2 schemas have no capability a seller can use to opt out, so this patch gates on what the seller publishes instead: the discovery read now asserts only a schema-valid `get_products` response and captures the returned products, and the policy-universe confirmation and the dual-violation `sync_creatives` phases run only when the first discovered product carries a non-empty `enforced_policies` list. The gate reads the seller's response, not the controller seed. A seller that omits the field, publishes an empty list, or returns no products under `required_policies` is not sent the violating creative and is not failed; its enforcement phases are skipped. A seller that publishes any enforced policy runs every step and is still graded on the exact two-policy universe and the one-`CREATIVE_REJECTED`-entry-per-policy result. No schema fields change. The 3.3 line gates the storyboard on the new `creative.supports_registry_policy_enforcement` capability instead (adcp#8857).
