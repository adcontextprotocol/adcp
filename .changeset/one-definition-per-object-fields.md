Values, ranges, and geographic objects that still drifted have one definition each. `core/open-ended-numeric-range.json` is the range with a minimum, a maximum, or both; the audience characteristic range, the capabilities `matching_latency_hours`, and the job salary range compose it, and the salary range requires `min` or `max`, as its description says. `core/distance.json` is a non-negative distance with its unit; `core/radius.json` and the vehicle mileage refine it. `core/plan-adjustment-amount.json` refines `core/money.json`, and the insertion order `total_budget` references it, so its currency is an ISO 4217 code. Postal address fields reference `core/address-street.json`, `core/address-city.json`, `core/address-region.json`, and `core/address-postal-code.json`, which take the bounds the business entity address published. Delivery rows at the country and region levels carry ISO 3166 codes in `geo_code`.

Collection, list, and installment identities have one definition each: the new `core/collection-id.json`, `core/canonical-collection-ref.json`, and `core/explicit-collection-selector.json`, with the existing `core/collection-list-id.json`, `core/property-list-id.json`, and `core/installment-id.json`, which the delete and update list tasks, `core/installment.json`, and the `get_collection_list` resolved entry now reference. The publisher property selector branches share one `publisher_domains` definition, the authorization violation code references `core/authorization-violation-code.json`, and the context match artifact type references `enums/content-artifact-identifier-type.json`. The delete and update list tasks reject an empty `list_id`; collection and installment IDs are non-empty and at most 255 characters; the resolved collection entry's `publisher_domain` is a lowercase domain name of at most 253 characters; installment `topics` and `guest_talent` are non-empty when present; and an echoed feature requirement's `allowed_values` is non-empty.

Account and agent fields have one definition each. The account-level notification config's event types reference `enums/account-anchored-notification-type.json`, the agent-level config and its readback share `core/agent-notification-event-types.json`, and the readback's authentication references `core/notification-authentication-state.json`, which composes `core/notification-authentication.json` and rejects `credentials`. Webhook and notification authentication share `core/webhook-auth-schemes.json`, and the governance agent's Bearer-only scheme list is `core/bearer-auth-schemes.json`. The verification token claims and the `agent.verification_earned` and `agent.verification_lost` registry events share `core/verification-badge-version.json`, and the token's `role` references `enums/verification-badge-role.json`. The operator on `sync_accounts` results, account candidates, and requested accounts is a `core/domain-name.json` domain, as on the account. The `indicators.changed` webhook IDs reference `core/account-id.json`, `core/media-buy-id.json`, `core/package-id.json`, and `core/creative-id.json`, so they are non-empty and the account and media buy IDs are at most 255 characters, and the sponsored context's paying-principal `account_id` references `core/account-id.json`. A badge event's `adcp_version` takes the token's MAJOR.MINOR form, and its `verified_specialisms` are unique. The agent notification config readback declares `signing_profile` with the config's signing profiles, and the buyer onboarding posture lists at most 16 requirements, as a seller's posture tier does.
