Compliance (experimental, `account.requests`): the new universal `account_requests` storyboard exercises `request_account` end to end. It opens a fresh sandbox account and reads the request back through `list_accounts` `request_ids`; checks the change rules every seller enforces (an `account_id` with `operator`, a money change without `revision`, a stale `revision`, `sandbox`, and an unreachable account that reads as unknown); and grades the seller-policy outcomes as branch sets: a separate account only with `allow_separate_account` and its distinct `ACCOUNT_CHANGE_REQUIRES_NEW_ACCOUNT` refusal, a change that resolves `matched` to an account the caller holds, and a buyer `sign_up` action completed with the `request_id`. When the seller advertises the `reporting_subscription_probe` controller scenario, it also checks reduced grants (`SCOPE_INSUFFICIENT`), revoked grants (the account is unknown), a re-grant that keeps `authorized_at` and records `regranted_at`, and a seller review that resolves on the sandbox clock. The training agent implements the surface on the 3.3 wire, and the training-agent storyboard matrix pins its result.
