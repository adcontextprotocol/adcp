Add experimental `list_collections` on the sales agent, under the new feature id `media_buy.collection_catalog` (#8183). It reads the seller's collection cards by key or with filters, and never returns products. A seller that lists the feature id must also list `media_buy.collection_cards`. The request takes `core/collection-selector.json` selectors (at most 100 collection IDs in total), filters, the shared `pagination` object, an optional `account`, and `if_collections_version`. The filters are `kinds`, `genres_include`, `genres_exclude`, `genre_taxonomy`, `statuses`, `languages` (RFC 4647 ranges), `talent`, `platforms` and `name_contains`. The request rejects unknown fields. The response is `listed` or `unchanged`, and carries `core/response-collection.json` copies, `collections_version` and `cache_scope`. Copies follow the #8141 authority rules unchanged. For the same key, account and moment, they equal the copy that `include_collections` returns. Unknown or invisible keys are omitted silently. `collections_version` is scoped to the selectors and filters, plus the account when `cache_scope` is `account`, and is independent of product feed versions. Callers below 3.3 get `UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE`.

Add three account-level webhook types: `collection_card.updated` (an upsert carrying the full card), `collection_card.removed` (the key only) and `collection_catalog.bulk_change` (no cards). They are added to `enums/notification-type.json`, the `notification-config.json` and `agent-notification-config.json` event lists, `wholesale_feed_webhooks.event_types`, the `core/wholesale-feed-webhook.json` envelope, and as new `core/wholesale-feed-event.json` branches, with `entity_type` gaining `collection`. They reuse the wholesale feed envelope, and `wholesale_feed_version` carries the unfiltered catalog's `collections_version`. Subscribers register through `sync_accounts` `notification_configs` and repair through `list_collections`. Listing any of the three types requires `media_buy.collection_catalog`, which the capabilities schema enforces.

A follower refresh that leaves the bounds and `precision` unchanged is not a change, so `distribution[].followers.as_of` now means when the count last changed, not when it was last read. The `core/collection-selector.json` description no longer says `adagents.json` is the only way to resolve a collection.

Add `collection-copies` test vectors with a reference grader. They grade the merged view of a copy and `adagents.json`, `list_collections` responses, webhook payloads, and mirror application.
