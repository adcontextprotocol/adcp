Add experimental `media_buy.measurement_provenance`: a shared `enums/collection-mechanism.json` vocabulary (`set_top_box`, `acr`, `panel`, `server_logs`, `client_tracker`, `sdk`) and two optional uses of it, both on open schemas (DR-0036). `measurement_terms.billing_measurement.counting_mechanisms[]` on `core/measurement-terms.json` declares what the billing count is counted from (sellers whose own system is the named vendor SHOULD declare it; absence means undeclared). `delivery-metrics.vendor_metric_provenance[]` (`core/vendor-metric-provenance.json`) carries provenance for a `vendor_metric_values[]` row as a sibling array joined on `(vendor, metric_id, qualifier)`: `mechanisms[]` and an optional `deduplicated`. The closed `canonical-measurement-terms.json` and `vendor-metric-value.json` row are unchanged; `counting_mechanisms` reaches the canonical terms in 4.0 (registry check `canonical_counting_mechanisms_required_in_4_0`). All new surfaces carry `x-status: experimental` and `x-added-in: "3.3.0"`, and sellers that emit them declare `media_buy.measurement_provenance` in `experimental_features`. A collection mechanism is not `measurement_source`, which keeps its meaning of provider slug. Broadcast as-run logs and station affidavits are airing evidence carried in `by_spot[]`, not a mechanism. Sellers serving a pinned older release should omit the new fields, since strict older validators reject them. `impression_id` joinability and `seller_tracker_events[]` are not part of this change.
