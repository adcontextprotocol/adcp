Add experimental minute-resolution daypart windows (#6707). `core/daypart-target.json` gains optional `start_time` / `end_time` (`HH:MM`, 24-hour, validated by pattern) beside the stable whole-hour `start_hour` / `end_hour`; an entry carries exactly one form (`oneOf` with `not`-required exclusion). Note for implementers: the stable `start_hour` / `end_hour` are no longer individually schema-required (one complete pair of either form is), so generated types make them optional and readers of echoed or proposed `daypart_targets` must not assume `start_hour` is present. An `end_time` earlier than `start_time` crosses midnight and belongs to the day it starts on; the entry's `timezone` clock semantics are unchanged. Products declare the finest honored resolution as `overlay_support.daypart_targets.time_granularity` (`hour` | `quarter_hour` | `minute`, new `enums/daypart-time-granularity.json`; default and legacy `true` mean `hour`), buyers can require a minimum through `required_overlay_support.daypart_targets.time_granularity` (ordered match), and a seller MUST reject, never round, a finer clock time with `UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE`. Marked `x-status: experimental` under feature id `media_buy.daypart_granularity`.
