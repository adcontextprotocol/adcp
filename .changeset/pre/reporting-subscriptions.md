Add experimental principal-level reporting subscriptions under the new feature id `media_buy.reporting_subscriptions` (RFC #8543, Part B). Reliable Reporting was configured one account at a time through `sync_accounts` `reporting_delivery_configs`, so every new account reported nothing until someone configured it. `sync_principal` gains a `reporting_subscriptions` section (`core/reporting-subscription.json`). A subscription has a `scope` of `all_authorized_accounts: true` (an explicit acknowledgment) or `account_ids[]`, optional `exclude_account_ids[]` and `operator_domains[]` filters, and a cross-seller `selector`: `feed_purpose`, `period_duration`, `required_finality`, an optional `alignment` filter, optional `dimensions[]`, and `match: exact | finest | all`. `match` defaults to `exact` when `dimensions` is present and `finest` when absent; unmapped native dimensions count as distinct, ties go to the lowest `offering_id`, and billing SHOULD use `exact`. `period_timezone` is allowed only for a subscription scoped to exactly one account. A subscription also carries `reconciliation_mode` and an optional `destination` naming a principal `reporting_destinations` entry; its `path_template` has no `.` or `..` segments, placeholders each fill exactly one percent-encoded segment, and it must contain `{account_id}` for multi-account scope or a shared destination. `subscription_version` changes for any semantic or scope change, and each version has its own `accepted_at`. `get_principal` and `sync_principal` read subscriptions back through `core/reporting-subscription-state.json`: `accepted_at`, `prior_versions`, `accounts_with_exceptions[]`, and `accounts_unauthorized[]`.

Coverage is computable by the buyer without asking. An account is covered while the scope selects it and the caller is authorized for it, meaning its grant lists `get_reporting_status`. Covered buys are the confirmed buys the caller sees through `get_media_buys`. Each (buy, resolved offering) pair is owed the complete periods of the offering's published schedule that start on or after the first boundary at or after the latest of the version's `accepted_at`, the buy's `confirmed_at`, and the account's new `authorization.authorized_at`. The periods must also overlap the buy's flight, from `start_time` to the earliest of `end_time`, `cancellation.canceled_at`, and the new optional media-buy `completed_at`, as of each period's `scope_resolved_at`. The period containing the stop time is owed in full; periods are never clipped, and nothing becomes owed retroactively. This refines RFC #8543 B1, which counted periods ending after a start that included authorization. Changes are cutovers at the instant T they take effect: the outgoing schedule owes its periods that start before T, in full, and the incoming schedule owes its periods that start at or after the end E of the last of them. No period is owed twice; when the schedules' boundaries don't align, the stretch from E to the next incoming boundary is owed under neither. Version changes, deactivation, overrides, `replace_account_configs`, resolution changes, and account timezone changes all use this rule. Each obligation's new optional `offering_id` is authoritative for its timing (`expected_at`).

`get_reporting_status` summary and periods views gain `subscription_coverage[]` (`core/reporting-subscription-coverage.json`). It reports only exceptions; each removes the periods whose start falls in `[from, until)`, and none adds one. `excluded[]` entries apply per account or buy, with a reason: `offering_unsupported` (optionally naming uncovered `package_ids`), `capacity`, `overridden` with the overriding id, `excluded_by_buyer`, or `action_required` with a `pending_action`. The view also carries `timezone_cutovers[]` and unbackfilled `gaps[]`. Precedence is caller-scoped: the caller's own active `sync_accounts` entry for an account and feed purpose wins over its subscriptions, and its `account_ids` subscription wins over its all-accounts one. `replace_account_configs: true` ends the caller's own matching account-level configurations on accounts where the subscription resolves, once per version; subscription writes never advance an account's `revision`. `action_required` exceptions are account-level only. `get_reporting_status` caller isolation is unchanged: lost authority appears in the subscription's `accounts_unauthorized[]` until re-grant or for `status_retention_days`, after which a re-grant is a fresh start. Withheld periods are owed again on re-grant, due at re-grant plus the offering's `delivery_sla`; with `backfill_on_regrant` no gap is recorded. `principal.changed` gains reason `reporting_subscription_changed` for revocations, re-grants, exceptions opening or closing, and a subscription moving to `action_required`. `sync_reporting_receipts` and `sync_reporting_status` keep accepting receipts and statuses for revisions read before `unauthorized_at`, for `status_retention_days`. `sync_accounts` rejects a `delivery_config_id` equal to one of the caller's `subscription_id` values.

Adds the extensible `enums/reporting-dimension.json` vocabulary (`package`, `creative`, `country`, `region`, `metro`). `media_buy.reporting_delivery` gains `configuration_tasks[]` (for example `["sync_principal"]` or `["sync_principal", "sync_accounts"]`) and an experimental `subscriptions` block (`supported`, `max_accounts`, `backfill_on_regrant`, `dimensions`). `configuration_task` keeps its `const: "sync_accounts"` value for clients pinned below 3.3 and is marked deprecated; it is now present when and only when `configuration_tasks` lists `sync_accounts` or is absent, and a seller listing only `sync_principal` serves reporting registration to 3.3 callers only. `adcp.principal.supported_sections` gains `reporting_subscriptions`. Advertising subscriptions requires the `media_buy.reporting_subscriptions` feature id, the `reporting_subscriptions` section, and `sync_principal` in `configuration_tasks`.
