A product's `publisher_properties` and `collections` rosters can now be undisclosed, with no new field. A non-empty list is the complete roster, as before; an empty list (`[]`) means the seller does not disclose it. Both lose `minItems: 1` on `product.json` and `canonical-product.json`, so generated types change for every product (a TypeScript non-empty tuple becomes `T[]`, a Pydantic `min_length` is dropped); SDKs must not index `[0]` unguarded and must treat `[]` as an unknown roster, not an empty set. There is no `partial` state: a seller that can name some publishers sells them as one product with a complete list. An absent list keeps its meaning: `publisher_properties` stays required on a `Product`, and an absent `collections` makes no collection declaration.

A seller serving a release below 3.3 MUST NOT return a product with an empty roster on request-bound discovery (DR-0024: the 3.2 schema requires one entry); wholesale feeds and webhooks are left undefined. Against an undisclosed roster, allow lists (`property_list`, `collection_list`, `collection_lists`) MAY be accepted and run only on the items the seller matches, with no promise to cover every listed item. Exclusion lists (`property_list_exclude`, `collection_list_exclude`, `collection_lists_exclude`) are the buyer's choice, since the buyer sees the undisclosed roster at discovery: a seller MAY accept one and MUST then apply it against its full internal roster, and a seller that cannot (it does not know every publisher it may serve) rejects it with `UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE` (correctable). Buyers and brand-safety logic SHOULD treat an undisclosed roster as opaque and MAY refuse such products; sellers SHOULD offer complete rosters where they can. Adds a versioning exceptions row, a migration section, and cross-SDK vectors under `static/test-vectors/product/`. Refs #6135.
