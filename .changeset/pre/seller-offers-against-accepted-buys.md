Add experimental seller offers against an accepted media buy (#8087; absorbs the offer-and-accept loop of #5683). `core/canonical-proposal.json` gains optional `proposal_initiator` (`buyer | seller`, new `enums/proposal-initiator.json`; absent means `buyer`). The reason lives outside the canonical terms: `list_proposals` rows gain `offer_reason` (new `enums/offer-reason.json`: `installment_postponed | installment_cancelled | preemption | under_delivery | seller_request`), required exactly when `origin` is `seller_offer`, and the `proposal.offered` payload carries it too. A seller-initiated proposal MUST have a `proposal_id` matching the notification id pattern and be a `media_buy_update` or `media_buy_cancellation` that is `committed` (`accepted` once the buyer accepts); the existing kind conditional still requires `parent_proposal_id`, `media_buy_id` and `base_media_buy_revision`. Offers are read through `list_proposals` and announced by the new account-anchored, identifiers-only `proposal.offered` notification (`core/proposal-offered-webhook.json`: `media_buy_id`, `proposal_id`, `expires_at`, `offer_reason`; `notification_id` equals `proposal_id`), a sibling of `proposal.created`; both depend on `media_buy.open_opportunities` (#8103) and a seller declaring `media_buy.seller_offers` MUST also declare `media_buy.open_opportunities` and advertise `list_proposals`. An offer carries no `opportunity_id`. `proposal.offered` is accepted by `notification-config.json` and `agent-notification-config.json`.

The buyer accepts with `accept_proposal`, declines with `decline_proposals`, or revises with `refine_proposals`, which yields a buyer-initiated draft. `expires_at` is the response deadline and the seller holds replacement inventory until then. At `expires_at` the offer's disposition is `expired`, `accept_proposal` returns `PROPOSAL_EXPIRED`, and the signed terms stand; silence is never acceptance. The cluster is `media_buy.seller_offers` in the experimental registry. Seller proposals into a buyer-opened open opportunity are `media_buy.open_opportunities` (#8096, #8103), not this surface.

Seller-held change terms (pre-agreed seller changes inside `commercial_terms.change_terms[]`) are not part of 3.3: `commercial_terms` and `change-term.json` stay closed for the whole 3.x major, so the design is recorded for 4.0 as the `seller_held_change_terms_required_in_4_0` check in the 4.0 compatibility registry and in a "Planned for 4.0" note in the proposal-negotiation guide (#8087). In 3.3 every seller-initiated change to a signed buy is an offer.

Compatibility: sellers MUST NOT send offers or `proposal.offered` notifications to callers served below AdCP 3.3, because `proposal_initiator` is a 3.3 field on a schema reopened in 3.3, and 3.3 fields on reopened schemas go only to 3.3 peers (DR-0036); a caller with no declared pin is treated as below 3.3.
