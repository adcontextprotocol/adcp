Add optional audience size fields to each `sync_audiences` audience result, so a seller can report what its platform actually reports instead of inventing a point value. `audience_size` is the people matched on the platform and `targetable_size` is the people the platform can serve now. Both use one new shared `core/audience-size.json` object: `lower_bound` and `upper_bound` (integers, at least 0, `upper_bound` at least `lower_bound`, equal when `precision` is `exact`), a `precision` of `exact`, `approximate`, or `bucketed` (new `enums/audience-size-precision.json`), and an optional `as_of` date-time. A new optional `match_rate_basis` (`platform_reported` or `derived`, new `enums/audience-match-rate-basis.json`) says where `effective_match_rate` came from. The existing `matched_count` and `effective_match_rate` keep their types and stay optional: the descriptions now say they MAY be absent when the seller cannot report them, and buyers MUST NOT read absence as zero. A seller MUST NOT disclose a size below its minimum, so the size fields are omitted when status is `too_small`, which stays governed by the existing `too_small` status and `minimum_size`. Additive and non-breaking: messages without the new fields are unchanged, and a 3.2 message is affected only if it already used `audience_size`, `targetable_size`, or `match_rate_basis` on an audience result with a different shape. Part 1 of #8023; audience overlap follows separately.
