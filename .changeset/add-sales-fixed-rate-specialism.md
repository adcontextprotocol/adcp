Add the `sales-fixed-rate` specialism for non-guaranteed, fixed-price sellers in any medium, digital or linear TV: no reserved inventory, no delivery commitment, a published fixed price (`fixed_price` present and no bid ever sent; CPM and CPP are pricing units, not bids), and an optional seller-review hold. Requested by two adopters: a fixed-price digital seller (sell.nofluffadvisory.com, #6303) and O-N-X (broadcast TV). A broadcast seller may claim both `sales-broadcast-tv` and `sales-fixed-rate`. The `sales_fixed_rate` storyboard reuses four fixed-price scenarios and adds capability discovery, `is_fixed_price` product discovery, and a bid-less `create_media_buy` that returns synchronous success with any valid status and `confirmed_at` set or null. Optional hold phases, gated on the new `force_media_buy_confirmation` `comply_test_controller` scenario (`action: hold | confirm`), grade confirmation, rejection, and buyer cancellation of a held buy by polling `get_media_buys`. Clarify that `status` is the buyer-actionable readiness axis and `confirmed_at` is the independent seller-commitment axis: the `pending_creatives` description no longer says the seller has accepted the buy, and the media-buy specification now covers provisional buys (revision on confirmation, idempotency replay, `pause`, and flight start before confirmation). Add the compliance catalog hold-path comparison, the training agent declaration. This is a normative narrowing: the existing rule that a seller MUST move a buy from `pending_start` to `active` when the flight date arrives now applies only to committed buys (`confirmed_at` non-null); a provisional buy stays `pending_start` until the seller confirms or rejects it.
