Docs: the 3.3 release notes, What's new, the 3.2 to 3.3 migration guide, the prerelease page, and Versions describe the reserved-word-safe enum replacements (#8915) as part of `3.3.0-rc.0`. `pass` becomes `passed`, `global` becomes `all`, and `name` becomes `full_name` in six positions. The originals are deprecated, not removed, and a replacement goes only to a peer served 3.3 or later.
