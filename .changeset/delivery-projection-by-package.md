Add an optional forward-looking `projection` to each top-level `by_package[]` row on `get_media_buy_delivery`, so a seller can share where a package's delivery is headed instead of every buyer re-deriving it from `daily_breakdown`. New `core/delivery-projection.json` carries `projected_impressions` and `projected_spend` (lifetime totals at flight end, both optional), a required `projected_end_state` (`on_pace`, `complete`, `underdelivery`, `early_exhaustion`), a required `basis` naming the method class, and a required `as_of`. New `enums/delivery-projection-basis.json` has four values: `trailing_daily_avg`, `trailing_hourly_avg`, `platform_model`, `seller_defined`; window length and model name go in `ext`. Projections are advisory forecasts, not delivery records: they carry no contractual meaning on their own, and any commercial use is governed by the parties' terms. Reported actuals remain the delivery record. `projection` is the numeric counterpart to the 3.2 `pacing_risk` indicator on `get_media_buys` and the forward counterpart to `pacing_index`; the docs explain how they compose. Additive and non-breaking: sellers that do not project omit the field and existing responses are unchanged. Not part of `windows[].by_package`, exact revision reads, or the `get_media_buys` snapshot, and this change does not define webhook use, and there is no buy-level roll-up. Stable rather than experimental: it is advisory and optional, and `basis` can gain values in later minors. Closes #5850.
