Add optional `budget.periods[]` to campaign governance plans so a budget can be partitioned by time as well as by purchase type. `sync_plans` accepts half-open `[start, end)` periods (`budget_period_id` with the new `governance_budget_period` x-entity, `start`, `end`, `amount`) that sit inside the plan flight, do not overlap, and sum to at most `budget.total`. `check_governance` derives the period containing a dated action's whole flight and denies a flight that straddles a boundary, sits in unallocated budget, or would take its period over its `amount` while the plan total has room. It returns the matched `budget_period_id`, accepts an optional `budget_period_id` assertion that is denied on mismatch, checks a modification that moves a buy as moving its whole commitment, and a re-sync that strands a dated commitment or cuts a period below what is committed is rejected with `VALIDATION_ERROR`. Undated commitments count against `budget.total` and allocations only. Adds the `governance.supports_budget_periods` capability, the `governance/budget_periods` storyboard (gated on it), and training-agent support. Plans without `budget.periods` behave exactly as before.
