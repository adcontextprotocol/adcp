Add `core/production-evidence-receipt.json`, an experimental claims payload for dated, scoped, issuer-signed evidence about what an AdCP implementation was observed doing (#7068, experimental feature id `governance.production_evidence`). The receipt has an evidence ladder (`conformance_passed` through `production_delivered_reconciled`), carries no spend, price, client, or counterparty identity by default, and is delivered through the existing `AttestationReference` rather than a new signing envelope. Issuer independence is declared and checkable: `issuer_counterparty_roles` is required, and from `independently_interoperable` upward the receipt also needs `counterparty_relation` and a digest-pinned `issuance_policy`, and a non-empty role declaration requires a co-signer that declared no roles. Co-signers present their own `production-evidence-endorsement` credential over a digest-pinned resource subject. An empty role declaration is a claim an evaluator must back with its own issuer policy. Adds the `production_evidence_receipt` x-entity type and a working-group draft at `specs/production-evidence-receipt.md`. Additive and experimental: no existing message changes. Referenced as #7068 until the working group decides placement and the independence definition.
