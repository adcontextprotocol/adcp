Add an optional, experimental, advisory `channels[]` and `excluded_channels[]` restriction (values from the existing `enums/channels.json`, mirroring the same file's `countries` and `excluded_countries`) to `core/rights-constraint.json`, so a rights grant can say where a licensed creative may run (#5542). Semantics: absent means no restriction; `excluded_channels` only means every channel except those excluded; `channels` is a positive grant, less anything also in `excluded_channels` (deny wins); an empty `channels` grants no channel; while `approval_status` is `pending` the entry grants no placement on any channel (the existing rule that a pending grant never authorizes serving; it applies to placement and serving, not to building). With several rights entries in force and relevant to an output, a channel must be permitted by every one. It is advisory, not enforcement: orchestrators and serving parties SHOULD NOT place the creative outside the effective set, and creative agents SHOULD check it against each requested output's channel in `build_creative` (a SHOULD in the task docs, not a MUST in the schema). A new advisory `RIGHTS_CHANNEL_VIOLATION` error code (on both `enumDescriptions` and `enumMetadata`) names a mismatch, and `BuildCreativeSuccess` and `BuildCreativeMultiSuccess` gain an optional non-terminal `errors[]` (as `BuildCreativeVariantSuccess` already has) to carry it on an otherwise-successful build; clients should dispatch on manifest presence rather than on `errors`, and the repository's discriminated-union error-handling idiom now says so. The field is covered by `content_digest` on attested constraints like any other serving-relevant field. A `platforms` axis and hard enforcement are deferred. Additive and non-breaking; listed under `brand.rights_lifecycle` in experimental status.
