Add `get_audience_overlap`, an optional and experimental read of how many people one audience a buyer synced shares with others on the same account. The request takes `account`, `audience_id`, and `compare_to`. Each comparison carries an `overlap_status`: `ready` with an `overlap` range that reuses `core/audience-size.json` and is always `approximate` or `bucketed`, `suppressed` when the seller withheld it, or `not_ready` while matching runs (new `enums/audience-overlap-status.json`). Sellers advertise `media_buy.audience_targeting.audience_overlap` with `max_comparisons` and `minimum_overlap_size` and list `media_buy.audience_overlap` in `experimental_features`. A seller MUST NOT disclose an overlap below its minimum, report it as exact, or add per-request randomness, MUST choose the reported range from the true overlap plus a seller-keyed deterministic offset, and MUST charge every comparison to a principal-scoped budget before evaluating suppression; suppressing near-duplicate audiences is a SHOULD. Only audiences the buyer uploaded through `sync_audiences` on the same account are comparable, and no share percentages are returned. Additive and non-breaking: the task, schemas, and capability block are new, and sellers that do not declare the capability are unchanged. Part 2 of #8023; builds on the audience size objects from part 1.
