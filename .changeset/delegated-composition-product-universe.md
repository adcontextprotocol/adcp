Add delegated-composition support for a requesting platform that owns the sellable catalog and asks a seller-operated agent to supply only composition judgment (#7107). `criteria.product_ids_exclusive` makes `product_ids` the complete composable universe for a request and its refinement lineage: every proposal purchase, echoed product, and seller-selected signal must resolve within it, an unsatisfiable brief returns `rejected` or `constraint_unsatisfiable`, a refinement may narrow but never widen it, and sellers not declaring the new `media_buy.supports_exclusive_product_ids` capability reject the flag with `UNSUPPORTED_FEATURE` instead of silently ignoring it. `media_buy.proposal_refinement.hold_semantics` (`inventory_hold` | `terms_only`) lets a seller that holds no inventory declare terms-only finalize: the same atomic all-or-none batch commits commercial terms without placing holds, `expires_at` is the acceptance window, `hold_unavailable` and hold-driven `batch_aborted` are excluded, and `accept_proposal` may decline with `PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE`. A `terms_only` seller must not return `committed` proposals to callers pinned below 3.3, and buyers must not send the universe flag to sellers that do not declare support. A delegated-composition bootstrap profile page and `get_adcp_capabilities` reference sections document both; the acceptance-only `accept_proposal` variant is deliberately not part of this change. Absent fields preserve today's behavior.
