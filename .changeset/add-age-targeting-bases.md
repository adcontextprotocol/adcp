Add basis-aware age targeting without weakening the canonical age predicate. Buyers can constrain `demographics.age.accepted_bases`, products declare user-level `supported_bases`, and package readback records effective applied bases and verification methods. Age compliance continues to override demographic permissions, and World ID threshold claims must entail the requested age predicate. This new capability should be reflected in future media-buy certification coverage.
