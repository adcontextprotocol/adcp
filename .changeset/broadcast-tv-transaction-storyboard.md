Refresh the `sales-broadcast-tv` compliance specialism (`sales_broadcast_tv` 1.1.0) so a passing run certifies one intentional, authorized TV transaction under agreed terms. The storyboard drops the "unit-based, not impression-based" framing, which predated `by_spot` and contradicted CPM-on-demo selling, and now walks five parts: discovery of `linear_tv` products with :15/:30 `video_hosted` formats, `cpm` pricing, a guaranteed forecast on a named demographic and `measurement_source`, and Live/C3/C7 windows; `TERMS_REJECTED` negotiation of `measurement_terms.billing_measurement` (currency vendor, window, variance); an authorized `create_media_buy` (signed by the runner where the seller declares `request_signing`); Live then C7 delivery reads; and reconciliation on the guarantee-basis window with `is_final` and `finalized_at`, plus `measurement_source` as an advisory check. Audience rows are read separately from as-run airing evidence.

Plan-to-as-run spot reporting (`by_spot` `scheduled`, `aired`, `preempted`, `makegood` with `replaces_spot_id`, break position) is a new optional phase gated on the seller listing the experimental `media_buy.spot_lifecycle` feature in `experimental_features`, so today's conformant sellers are not failed and skip it as not applicable. Spot declarations are per product and cannot gate a runner step, so the phase seeds its own spot-reporting product and buys it separately. Schema validity of every returned row and agreement of the package's `plays` with the injected plays are required. Whether the seller reports a scheduled row, a stable `spot_id` and a break position is an advisory observation, because no controller scenario defines how simulated delivery maps to spot rows and a seller listing `scheduled` does not promise a planning horizon.

This tightens the specialism for existing claimants, and they should expect it. The storyboard's controller-seeded fixtures change: products are CPM-priced with a guaranteed forecast (demographic, demographic system, `measurement_source` advisory) and Live/C3/C7 measurement windows, and the buy assigns creatives through `sync_creatives` `assignment_operations` instead of inline `creative_assignments`. `get_products` now must return the seeded `cpm` options, `linear_tv` channel, :15 and :30 `duration_ms_exact` formats, the forecast and a C7 window, and delivery reads must report `measurement_window`, `is_final` and `finalized_at` for the Live and C7 reads, as `media_buy_seller/billing_finality_delivery` already requires. A seller whose `seed_product` drops `forecast` or `measurement_windows` will newly fail. The storyboard also adds a `TERMS_REJECTED` negotiation step that mirrors the already-required `media_buy_seller/measurement_terms_rejected` scenario. Delivery-window webhook grading is unchanged except that it now runs last, so a missing webhook no longer cascades into the delivery and reconciliation steps. The training agent's `/sales` tenant now declares `sales-broadcast-tv`.
