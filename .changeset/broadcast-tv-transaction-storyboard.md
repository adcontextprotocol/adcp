Refresh the `sales-broadcast-tv` compliance specialism (`sales_broadcast_tv` 1.1.0) so a passing run certifies one intentional, authorized TV transaction under agreed terms. Guaranteed delivery is not required to earn the badge: linear TV is also sold at a fixed price the seller sets, with no reserved inventory, no guarantee and no bid. The storyboard covers both paths, and a seller passes by completing at least one. The path phases are optional and an `assert_contribution` gate fails only a seller that completes neither. The "unit-based, not impression-based" framing, which predated `by_spot` and contradicted CPM-on-demo selling, is gone.

Shared discovery checks `linear_tv` products with :15/:30 `video_hosted` formats, a demo-priced option (`cpm` or `cpp`), a forecast naming its demographic and `measurement_source` (advisory), and Live/C3/C7 windows. The guaranteed path adds a guaranteed-product read, `TERMS_REJECTED` negotiation of `measurement_terms.billing_measurement`, an authorized `create_media_buy` (signed by the runner where the seller declares `request_signing`), `sync_creatives` with `assignment_operations`, Live then C7 delivery reads, and reconciliation on the agreed window with `is_final` and `finalized_at`. The fixed-price path asserts a non-guaranteed product with a `fixed_price` option and no `floor_price`, `price_guidance` or `max_bid`, buys it with measurement terms that evaluate rather than guarantee delivery, and reads delivery on the agreed window. An optional seller-approval step (hold, then confirm) runs only when the seller advertises the `force_media_buy_confirmation` controller scenario proposed with `sales-fixed-rate`, and is skipped otherwise. Audience rows are read separately from as-run airing evidence.

Plan-to-as-run spot reporting (`by_spot` `scheduled`, `aired`, `preempted`, `makegood` with `replaces_spot_id`, break position) is a new optional phase gated on the seller listing the experimental `media_buy.spot_lifecycle` feature in `experimental_features`, so today's conformant sellers are not failed and skip it as not applicable. Spot declarations are per product and cannot gate a runner step, so the phase seeds its own spot-reporting product and buys it separately. Schema validity of every returned row and agreement of the package's `plays` with the injected plays are required. Whether the seller reports a scheduled row, a stable `spot_id` and a break position is an advisory observation, because no controller scenario defines how simulated delivery maps to spot rows and a seller listing `scheduled` does not promise a planning horizon.

This tightens the specialism for existing claimants, and they should expect it. The controller-seeded fixtures change: products are demo-priced `cpm` with Live/C3/C7 measurement windows, and the buy assigns creatives through `sync_creatives` `assignment_operations` instead of inline `creative_assignments`. A seller must return the products it seeds (and complete at least one path), and delivery reads must report `measurement_window`, `is_final` and `finalized_at` for the Live and C7 reads, as `media_buy_seller/billing_finality_delivery` already requires. A seller whose `seed_product` drops `forecast` or `measurement_windows` will newly fail. The storyboard also adds a `TERMS_REJECTED` negotiation step that mirrors the already-required `media_buy_seller/measurement_terms_rejected` scenario. Delivery-window webhook grading is unchanged except that it runs after the other phases, so a missing webhook no longer cascades into the delivery and reconciliation steps. Claims graded on `sales_broadcast_tv` 1.0.x have a grace window recorded in the compliance catalog. The training agent's `/sales` tenant now declares `sales-broadcast-tv`.
