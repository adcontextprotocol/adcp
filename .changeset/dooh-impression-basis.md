Add the experimental DOOH impression basis (feature id `media_buy.dooh_impression_basis`, RFC #7253): a shared `enums/impression-estimation-basis.json` tier enum (`currency_measured`, `seller_modeled`, `sensor_measured`, `hybrid`) that says what kind of number a DOOH `impressions` figure (`plays × audience multiplier`) is. Delivery rows carry `dooh_metrics.impression_basis`, the declared `audience_multiplier`, a `basis_metric` pointer to the `vendor_metric_values[]` row a sensor audience comes from, and, for a row whose plays were estimated on different tiers, an `impression_basis_breakdown[]` (`core/impression-basis-breakdown-entry.json`) of at least two entries with distinct tiers, which sum exactly to the row's plays and impressions and replace the row-level tier (a row never carries both) and the blended multiplier; `venue_breakdown[]` rows may carry the tier and multiplier. Forecast points carry the same declaration and the planning multiplier outside `metrics`, a forecast breakdown including the fallback the seller expects, and product and canonical `dooh_placement_attributes` the planned tier and multiplier. `hybrid` is a seller model calibrated by the seller's own sensors, never a published currency nor a mix of tiers. All fields are optional; `ooh_metrics.estimation_basis` and the closed canonical forecast schemas are unchanged.
