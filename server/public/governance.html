Transition to an elected board

The launch transitional period ends at the earlier of the first annual member meeting at which voting members elect directors or June 11, 2027, twelve months after adoption of the bylaws. The first annual member meeting to elect directors must take place by the end of that period; its date will be set by the board.

The expanded initial board held its first regular meeting on August 6, 2026 for reporting and community building. No business matters were proposed or resolved. Its minutes await board review and approval.

Board structure

During the launch transitional period, the initial board may appoint successor or additional directors and must have 3–41 directors (Section 4.02(a)). After the transition, the board will have 5–41 directors, including the CEO if one is appointed, with its size fixed by the board (Section 4.02(b)).

After the transition, category-designated seats must represent marketers, marketing agencies, publishers, and technology providers equally, or as nearly equally as practicable. Voting members elect directors for their own category. Each director must be a current employee of a qualified voting member in the appropriate category or an officer of the organization.

Director terms after the transition

  • Directors serve 3-year terms
  • Terms are staggered so ~1/3 of the board is elected each year
  • Maximum of 2 consecutive elected terms, then a 1-year break before re-election
  • Initial board service is limited to the launch transitional period and does not count toward the elected-term limit

Membership classes and voting categories

The bylaws establish three membership classes: voting members, individual members, and associate members. Individual and associate members do not have voting rights. Voting members form a single membership class and are classified by the board into four industry categories (Section 3.02).

Marketers

Producers of goods and services for consumers or other businesses.

Marketing agencies

Suppliers of strategic, creative, procurement, or data services to marketers.

Publishers

Creators of original content for consumers or other businesses whose revenue derives significantly from marketing or advertising programs sold to marketers.

Technology providers

Developers and suppliers of agentic services for participants in the marketing and media supply chain.

Organizations that qualify for multiple categories may request one category when applying; the board determines classification. A voting member may request a category change no more often than once every 24 months, with prior board approval. Each voting member has one vote through its designated representative, subject to the shared-vote rules for affiliated entities in Section 3.02(a)(iii).

  • Meeting records - Board actions are recorded; minutes awaiting approval are identified as such
  • Public bylaws - Our governing documents are available to everyone
  • Financial transparency - Annual financial reports will be shared with membership
  • Balanced representation - Elected board seats follow the category parity requirement in the bylaws

Delaware incorporation

AgenticAdvertising.org is incorporated in Delaware. Its governance is subject to its certificate of incorporation, bylaws, and applicable law, including the Delaware General Corporation Law.

Get involved

There are several ways to participate in AgenticAdvertising.org:

Serve on the board

After the launch transition, category-designated directors are nominated from and elected by their voting member category. Directors must be current employees of qualified voting members in the appropriate category or officers of the organization.

Join a working group

Working groups shape protocol development and organizational priorities. Members may participate in groups open to their membership class, subject to applicable participation rules.

Become a member

Voting membership is open to qualified organizations in the four industry categories. Individual and associate memberships offer participation without voting rights. See our members or learn how to join.
